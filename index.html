02 / OUR APPROACH

Better tools.
Considered decisions.

Software can make information easier to work with. Our approach keeps the source material close and people responsible for the decisions that matter.

↳ Built around the work.
Guided by human judgment.
  1. 01

    Start with the source.

    Ground research and summaries in source information so the reasoning can be checked.

  2. 02

    Make quality part of the process.

    Build in checks for missing information, inconsistencies, and details that need a closer look.

  3. 03

    Keep people in control.

    Sourcing, pricing, and business commitments remain matters of human judgment.

  4. 04

    Measure what makes work better.

    Evaluate improvements through accuracy, review time, and operating cost.

CURRENT DEVELOPMENT

Starting with a
practical foundation.

Early development

Our groundwork includes a DIBBS data-collection prototype covering daily and historical procurement records. The initial focus is an internal workflow for evaluating opportunities and preparing sourcing decisions.

Next, we are exploring how Claude could support document extraction, summaries grounded in source material, and supplier research. This integration is planned, with important decisions kept under human review.
